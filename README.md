## Personal Ethics Statement

*In chapter 5 of Ethical Machines, Reid Blackman talks about companies that suffered damage due to "ethics washing" in their AI written algorithms, which led to many issues with privacy and security.*

AI written code may contain security vulnerabilities because of the data it was trained on, which the developer may have overlooked or not even checked for. This can lead to data breaches and other possible attacks. Also, AI has a tendency to misread human ideas due to their vagueness, resulting in cases where users’ privacy and ethical security are violated. And due to that companies can gain access to user's personal information, which they most likely didn’t want to share. But it's what the companies and corporations have already been doing for years. They intentionally collect, sell, and use people’s data for their own profit and benefit. So the main problem here isn't the AI's inability to judge morally or "ethics washing", rather the problem is that most companies have intentionally been collecting our data for years, violating our privacy in their own gain.
